description=[b]PZ Mod Studio[/b] is a free, open-source program for Windows that manages your Project Zomboid (Build 42) mods: profiles, load order, error diagnosis, conflicts between mods, save backups and dedicated servers. [b]This mod, the Link, connects it to the game[/b], so what the app knows shows up where you actually play: on the main menu, on the Mods screen and before a save loads.

description=[list]
description=[*][b]What's new[/b]: your mods updated since you last played, with their change notes, the changelog each author ships, and which ones may break saves.
description=[*][b]Mod authors come first[/b]: their Ko-fi, Patreon and other support links shown beside each of their mods; error reports ready to send them; their mods never edited or re-uploaded.
description=[*][b]Browse[/b]: the Steam Workshop and Nexus inside the app; subscribe to a whole collection in one click.
description=[*][b]Community lists[/b]: mod lists shared by other players, copied into yours in one click, and an index of known issues and their fixes. Both on the [url=https://pichu6.github.io/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/]community index[/url].
description=[*][b]Maps[/b]: every map mod drawn on the real map of Knox Country, and where two of them claim the same ground.
description=[*][b]Servers[/b]: find a server and make a profile with its mods, or host a dedicated server with yours, with a Discord channel and bot.
description=[*][b]For modders[/b]: save a .lua and it is checked and running in the game in about 0.3 s, without restarting or losing the game's state. Also: create a mod, check its files before the game does, a multiplayer check.
description=[*][b]For AI assistants[/b]: an MCP server, so an assistant can read your logs and mod list and help you fix an error.
description=[/list]

description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/banners/s2-game.png[/img]
description=[h2]With the app open[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]The active profile on the Mods screen.[/b] What you turn on or off in the game goes back to the profile; new subscriptions show as "+N new".
description=[*][b]Mod Health marks[/b] on the mods with a problem, with the reason, and your notes on each mod.
description=[*][b]A card on the main menu[/b]: the active profile, how many problems Mod Health found, and what changed in the save Continue would load.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]A check before every save loads[/h2]
description=Loading a save with different mods can lose items, vehicles or map areas for good. So when a save's mods changed since you last played it (added, taken out, updated, reordered), the Link shows what changed and you choose: [b]Load it[/b] (the app backs it up first), [b]Play on a copy[/b], or [b]Back[/b]. A save that uses mods outside the active profile does not load by mistake.
description=
description=[h2]Even without the app[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]An error notice[/b]: when a mod's Lua fails, it names the mod (and the one that called it). Copy report puts the details on the clipboard for the mod's author.
description=[*][b]A clearer Mods screen[/b]: search, filters, a mod comes on with what it needs, a load order you can drag. The game's screen is one button away.
description=[*]A small card on the main menu about the app. Hide removes it for good.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/banners/s3-safe.png[/img]
description=[b]The mod[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Only Lua: five plain-text files you can read in the mod's folder. No Java, no DLLs, nothing compiled.
description=[*]It writes only what the game lets any mod write: its own small files in Zomboid/Lua, and your mod list, the same way the game's screen does.
description=[*]It cannot start programs or open web pages ("Download" copies the address), and it sends nothing anywhere.
description=[/list]
description=[b]The app[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Open source under the MIT license: every line of the app and of this mod is on GitHub, to read or to build yourself.
description=[*]No account, no telemetry, no ads. It goes online only to browse the Workshop or Nexus, to fetch the community rules once a day (can be switched off), and to GitHub if you propose a rule.
description=[*]It never edits Workshop mods: fixes are separate mod folders you can delete. Your mod list is backed up every time it changes.
description=[*]A save is only changed when you say yes, and after a backup. The one game file it can touch is a Java-agent mod's launch line in ProjectZomboid64.json, backed up each time; Steam's "Verify integrity" restores it.
description=[*]Windows may show a SmartScreen notice the first time only because the program has no paid code-signing certificate.
description=[/list]
