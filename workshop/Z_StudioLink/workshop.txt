description=
description=[h2]With the PZ Mod Studio app[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The Mods screen shows the profile active in the app first; mods subscribed outside it can be added in one click.
description=[*]The mods Mod Health warns about are marked, with the reason.
description=[*]The mods updated on the Workshop since your last session, and the ones Steam has waiting.
description=[*]Your own notes on mods and your own groups (made in the app), with a group turned on or off in one click.
description=[*]Before a save loads: what changed in its mods since its last session, a backup first, or play on a copy.
description=[*]A save that uses mods outside the profile does not load by mistake.
description=[*]A card in the main menu: the profile, Mod Health and the save Continue would load.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]On its own (no app needed)[/h2]
description=A clearer Mods screen, opened by the main menu's MODS button:
description=[list]
description=[*]Search, and filters: on, off, problems, maps.
description=[*]Each mod's poster, description, version, what it requires (on, off or not installed) and what needs it.
description=[*]Turning a mod on brings what it requires with it; turning one off says first which mods need it.
description=[*]Favourites (the same ones the game's screen keeps) and the load order: sort by it, drag mods or move them up and down (never above what they require).
description=[*]Several mods at once (Ctrl / Shift + click), groups by author or category, a summary on top, and each mod's options page.
description=[*]The map order, undo and redo, and a list / details split you can drag.
description=[*]Accept and Back like the game's screen, which stays one button away.
description=[/list]
description=No save is touched. The main menu shows where to get the app; that card can be hidden.
description=
description=[h2]Other mod managers[/h2]
description=If another mod manager that draws the Mods screen is on (DMM, PZMM...), the game asks once which one to use. Save backups and checks stay either way.
