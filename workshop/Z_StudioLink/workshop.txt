description=[h1]A Mods screen that tells you what is going on[/h1]
description=The game's Mods screen is fine with 10 mods. With 150 it is a wall of names: you can't search it, you don't see what a mod needs, and you find out a library was missing when the game is already loading.
description=
description=[b]PZ Mod Studio Link[/b] replaces it with a screen built for big mod lists. Subscribe, enable it once in the game's Mods list, and from then on the main menu's [b]MODS[/b] button opens the new screen. Nothing else is needed: it works on its own, no app, no setup.
description=
description=[h2]Find any mod in a second[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/02-search-filters.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Search by name, mod id, Workshop id or author.
description=[*]Filters: [b]On[/b], [b]Off[/b], [b]Problems[/b] (mods the game can't load on your build, or that miss something), [b]Maps[/b] and [b]Favourites[/b].
description=[*]Group the list by author or category (maps, vehicles, features, modpacks) and fold the groups.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Requirements, handled[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/03-requirements.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Turn a mod on and everything it requires comes on with it. If something isn't installed, you're told which one, before you start a game.
description=[*]Turn a library off and you see first which mods need it: they go off with it, or you go back.
description=[*]Each mod shows its poster, description, version, the builds it supports, what it requires (on, off or not installed), what needs it, and what it loads after or before.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]A load order you can actually manage[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/04-load-order.gif[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Sort by load order and drag mods, or move them up and down.
description=[*]A mod never goes above what it requires: it stops where it can go and says why.
description=[*]Map order for map mods that share a place.
description=[*]Undo and redo for everything, until you press Accept.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Many mods at once[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/05-many-mods.gif[/img]
description=Ctrl+click and Shift+click to pick several mods, then turn them all on or off, or favourite them. Each mod's options open in a window over the list.
description=
description=[h2]Which mod threw that error?[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/06-error-notice.png[/img]
description=When a Lua error happens, in the menu or in game, a small notice names the mod it came from (and which mod called it). [b]Copy report[/b] puts the error on your clipboard, ready to paste on the mod's page. [b]Show in Mods[/b] opens that mod, to turn it off.
description=
description=[h2]Safe by design[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]It writes your mod list the same way the game's screen does. No save is ever touched.
description=[*]The game's own screen is always one button away.
description=[*]Other mod managers (DMM, PZMM...): the game asks once which one to use.
description=[/list]
description=

description=[h1]Even more with PZ Mod Studio (free app for Windows)[/h1]
description=The Link is the in-game side of [b]PZ Mod Studio[/b], a free, open-source mod manager for Project Zomboid. You don't need it to use this mod. But if you play with many mods, it does what a game screen can't:
description=
description=[h2]Profiles: a whole mod setup in one click[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/07-profiles.gif[/img]
description=Keep a "vanilla+" setup and a "200 mods" setup without unsubscribing from anything. Switch in the app and the game's Mods screen shows that profile. New subscriptions show as "+N new" to add in one click.
description=
description=[h2]Your saves, protected[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/08-save-check.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*]Before a save loads you see what changed in its mods since you last played: updated, added, taken out.
description=[*]Then: load it (with a backup first), play on a copy, or go back.
description=[*]A save never loads by mistake with mods outside its profile, and a save with an empty mod list doesn't lose its modded items.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Mod Health[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/docs/forum/images/04-mod-health.png[/img]
description=Your mod list checked with the game's own rules: mods your build can't load, missing requirements, wrong order, incompatible pairs. Each problem with a fix button, and its mark on the in-game screen.
