description=[h2]With the PZ Mod Studio app[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The Mods screen shows the profile active in the app; mods subscribed outside it can be added in one click.
description=[*]The mods Mod Health warns about are marked, with the reason on hover.
description=[*]Before a save loads: what changed in its mods since its last session, a backup first, or play on a copy.
description=[*]A save that uses mods outside the profile does not load by mistake.
description=[*]A card in the main menu: the profile, Mod Health and the save Continue would load.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]On its own[/h2]
description=Without the app nothing of the game changes: no screen is replaced and no save is touched. The main menu shows where to get the app, and that card can be hidden.
description=
description=[h2]Other mod managers[/h2]
description=If another mod manager that draws the Mods screen is on (DMM, PZMM...), the game asks once which one to use. Save backups and checks stay either way.
description=
