description=[h1]Your mods and your saves, looked after from inside the game[/h1]
description=[b]PZ Mod Studio Link[/b] is the in-game side of [b]PZ Mod Studio[/b], a free, open-source mod manager for Windows. The app does the heavy work outside the game; the Link brings it into the game: your profile on the Mods screen, a check before every save loads, the mod behind every error, and Mod Health's warnings right where you turn mods on.
description=
description=[h2]Which mod broke? The game tells you[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/06-error-notice.png[/img]
description=When a Lua error happens, in the menu or in game, a small notice names the mod it came from and the mod that called it, read from the error itself. No digging through console.txt.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Copy report[/b]: the error, the mods and the stack on your clipboard, ready for the mod author.
description=[*][b]Show in Mods[/b]: that mod, picked, to turn it off with what depends on it.
description=[*][b]See in the Studio[/b] (with the app): the error's card, with what usually fixes it.
description=[/list]
description=Works on its own, without the app.
description=
description=[h2]A save never loads with the wrong mods[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/08-save-check.png[/img]
description=With the app, every save is checked before it loads:
description=[list]
description=[*]What changed in its mods since you last played it: updated on the Workshop, added, taken out, a different load order. A map mod taken out is called out, because what it built would be gone.
description=[*]Then you choose: [b]Load it[/b] (a backup is made first), [b]Play on a copy[/b] (the save stays as it was), or [b]Back[/b].
description=[*]A save that uses mods outside the active profile does not load by mistake.
description=[*]A save whose mod list came out empty gets its mods back instead of losing every modded item.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Profiles the game follows[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/workshop/Z_StudioLink/media/09-profile-in-game.png[/img]
description=Keep a light setup and a 200-mod setup side by side, without unsubscribing from anything. Switch profile in the app (top of this page) and the game's Mods screen shows that profile; mods you turn on or off in the game go back to the profile.
description=[list]
description=[*]Subscribed to something new? It shows as [b]+N new[/b], one click to add it to the profile.
description=[*]Your own notes and groups of mods, made in the app, shown on each mod in the game.
description=[*]A card in the main menu: the active profile, Mod Health and the save Continue would load.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Mod Health, where you turn mods on[/h2]
description=[img]https://github.com/Pichu6/-Project-Zomboid-Mod-Studio-PZ-Mod-Studio-/raw/main/docs/forum/images/04-mod-health.png[/img]
description=The app checks your list with the game's own rules: mods your build can't load, missing requirements, wrong order, incompatible pairs, each with a fix. In the game, the same mods carry the mark and the reason, and the Problems filter lists them.
description=
description=[h2]Two mods change the same file? Both work[/h2]
description=The app's [b]Mod Merger[/b] finds mods that overwrite each other's files and builds one fix package that merges them, so neither stops working in silence. The Link keeps that package loading last.
description=

description=[/list]
description=Your mod list is written the way the game writes it, and the game's own screen is one button away.
